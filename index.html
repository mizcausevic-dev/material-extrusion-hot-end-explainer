System boundary · conservation

Mass in: solid filament

Mass out: deposited bead + part. No mass is created in the nozzle.

Energy in: electrical energy to heater

Energy out/stored: warms polymer, losses via heat sink & fans, bead cools. Split is inferred, not measured.

Charge: circuits stay inside equipment boundary. No charge pathway is drawn into the polymer.
